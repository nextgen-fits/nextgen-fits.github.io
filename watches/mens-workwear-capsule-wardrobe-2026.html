Frequently Asked Questions

Why buy an analog or solar tool watch over a smartwatch in 2026?

Smartwatches create digital distraction, require daily battery charging, and face software obsolescence within 3 years. An analog or solar tool watch never needs a wall charger, handles mud and vibration without glitching, and develops personal patina over decades.

Is 100 meters of water resistance safe for regular swimming?

Yes. 30m is splash-proof (rain/hand washing), 50m handles brief immersion, while 100m (10 ATM) is the certified benchmark for everyday recreational swimming and water sports. For actual scuba diving, choose a 200m watch with a screw-down crown like the Casio Duro, G-Shock, or Invicta Pro Diver.

How long do solar watches like Citizen Eco-Drive and G-Shock Tough Solar last?

Modern solar capacitors are engineered to retain over 80% of their operational capacity after 20 years of continuous use. They charge under ordinary indoor lighting as well as direct sunlight, making them virtually maintenance-free.
