Frequently Asked Questions

What's the best watch under $100 overall?

For sheer toughness, the Casio G-Shock DW5600E-1V at under $30 is unbeatable. For dive-watch aesthetics and 200m water resistance, the Casio Duro MDV106-1AV remains an undisputed value champion.

Can you get a real automatic (mechanical) watch under $100?

Yes. The Invicta Pro Diver 8926OB is regularly available for around $65 and houses Seiko's reliable NH35A automatic movement. The iconic Seiko 5 SNK809 mechanical field watch, while traditionally sub-$100, now typically trades between $100 and $200 due to collector demand following its discontinuation.

Is Invicta a good brand, or is the quality control reputation deserved?

The NH35A automatic movement inside the Invicta Pro Diver is genuinely reliable — it's the same movement respected microbrands charge $300+ for. However, Invicta's quality control can be variable. Inspect your unit upon delivery and take advantage of Amazon's return window if anything feels off.

Do budget watches keep accurate time?

Quartz watches (Casio, Timex, Fossil) are extremely precise — typically within 15-30 seconds a month. Automatic mechanical watches (Seiko, Invicta) rely on mechanical gear trains and typically drift a few seconds to a minute per day, which is inherent to mechanical horology.
