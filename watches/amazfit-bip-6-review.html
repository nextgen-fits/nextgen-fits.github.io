Fitness & Health Tracking Performance

With 140+ workout modes, the Bip 6 covers everything from outdoor running to HYROX training and strength workouts. The built-in GPS locks on quickly in outdoor environments, giving reliable distance and pace data. Through the Zepp App, you can also download offline maps directly to your wrist for turn-by-turn routing during hikes or trail runs.

The BioTracker 6.0 sensor performs admirably for 24/7 heart rate monitoring, blood oxygen tracking, and sleep stage analysis. One-Tap Measuring allows you to capture heart rate, SpO2, stress, and respiration metrics simultaneously in under 45 seconds.

Real-World Battery Life

Amazfit advertises a 14-day battery life. In our real-world testing with continuous health monitoring, notifications, and 45 minutes of daily GPS workout tracking, the Bip 6 consistently delivered 6 to 8 days on a single charge. While heavy GPS users won't hit the full 14 days, lasting a full week effortlessly crushes competitors like the Apple Watch SE, which requires daily charging.
