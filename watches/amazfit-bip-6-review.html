3. Battery Reality Check: Ending "Daily Charger Anxiety"

The single biggest frustration among flagship smartwatch owners is Daily Charger Anxiety. Having your watch die halfway through an overnight flight or evening workout is frustrating.

Amazfit’s published scenarios are up to 14 days of typical use, up to 6 days of heavy use, and up to 26 days in battery-saver mode. These are manufacturer estimates; GPS, screen use, calls and monitoring settings change runtime.

4. Decision Guide: Who Should (and Shouldn't) Buy This?

BUY THIS IF YOU WANT:
  • Crisp 1.97" AMOLED screen that pops in sunlight
  • a battery estimate of up to 14 days in typical use (manufacturer claim)
  • built-in GPS for runs without a phone
  • 140+ workout modes with auto-strength detection
  • lightweight aluminum-alloy frame
SKIP THIS IF YOU NEED:
  • NFC mobile payments (Apple Pay/Google Pay)
  • Advanced medical ECG hardware sensors
  • Extensive third-party app stores
  • A heavy stainless steel case feel
