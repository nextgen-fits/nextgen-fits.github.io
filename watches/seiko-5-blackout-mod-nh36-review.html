When you strip away the custom dials and ceramic inserts, the beating heart of every Seiko 5 build is the TMI NH36 (or factory Seiko 4R36). Manufactured by Time Module Inc. 2. The Blackout Architecture: What Actually Changes?

A true custom blackout build isn't merely an all-black watch off a store shelf; it is an exercise in material refinement. We modified the factory SRPD platform with three crucial aftermarket upgrades:

Upgrade 1: Double-Domed Sapphire Crystal with Blue Anti-Reflective (AR) Coating

The single biggest flaw of factory Seiko 5 watches is their proprietary Hardlex mineral crystal. While Hardlex resists shattering upon heavy impact, it scratches easily when scraped against doorknobs or car keys. Upgrading to a double-domed sapphire crystal provides a Mohs hardness rating of 9 (diamond is 10). The double-dome geometry eliminates optical dial distortion at steep viewing angles while the underside AR coating prevents annoying light glare on the matte black dial.

Upgrade 2: Matte Ceramic Bezel Insert (Sub-Style Typography)

The standard factory aluminum bezel insert is prone to fading and edge-scuffs. Replacing it with a sintered matte black ceramic insert elevates the watch into luxury territory. Ceramic is virtually impervious to scratches and UV degradation, and the recessed engraved markers create striking visual depth.

Upgrade 3: Molecular Black PVD Coating

Physical Vapor Deposition (PVD) is applied inside a high-vacuum chamber where titanium-nitride particles bond directly to the 316L stainless steel. The resulting satin-matte texture reduces reflections, resists fingertip smudges, and creates a unified tactical presence.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: What is the difference between the Seiko 4R36 and the unbranded NH36 movement?

The Seiko 4R36 and NH36 are mechanically identical movements manufactured by Time Module Inc. (TMI), a Seiko subsidiary. The 4R36 is stamped for official Seiko-branded retail watches, while the NH36 is sold to microbrands and independent modders. Both feature 24 jewels, 21,600 vph, hand-winding, and hacking seconds.

Q2: Does swapping the crystal compromise the watch's 100m water resistance?

If installed correctly with a fresh nylon I-gasket and pressed evenly using a dedicated crystal press tool, water resistance remains intact. Our lab hydro-pressure testing verified solid 10-bar (100m) resistance on properly seated double-domed sapphire upgrades.

Q3: Is the black PVD coating durable against everyday scratches?

Physical Vapor Deposition (PVD) bonds at a molecular level with the 316L stainless steel. It resists minor surface abrasions, desk rubbing, and sweat corrosion exceptionally well, though deep impacts against rough stone or metal can reveal the raw steel beneath.

It provides the single most accessible gateway into horology: a platform that will reliably tick for the next decade, with parts that can be repaired on any kitchen table with a $15 toolkit.
