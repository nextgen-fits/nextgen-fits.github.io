What to compare first

A GMT complication generally adds a 24-hour hand or equivalent indication so a traveler can keep home time visible while changing local time. Some movements let the local hour jump independently; others require a different adjustment. Read the exact instructions because “GMT” does not guarantee the same setting experience.

Frequently asked questions

What does a GMT watch do?

It can display an additional time zone using a 24-hour indication; setting behavior differs by movement.

Is world time better than GMT?

It depends: world time offers more zones at once, while GMT often keeps a cleaner dial and one tracked zone.

Can these watches handle daylight-saving changes?

Most mechanical displays do not automatically adjust. Confirm time-zone rules and reset when needed.
