How our links work ↗
Is the product plan really free?

Yes. There is no planning fee and no obligation to buy. Tell us your budget and needs, and we can help put together a practical product shortlist.

What details make a recommendation more useful?

Your budget, country or region, intended use and anything you already own. For a watch, wrist size helps. For a desk setup, your laptop model, monitor needs and available space are useful.

Do I need an account?

No. An email address is enough to request the newsletter. Choose the free product plan option to add the details that will help us recommend suitable products.

How do affiliate links work?

Some shopping links are affiliate links. If you buy through them, NextGen may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We explain model fit, limitations and alternatives so you can make the decision.
