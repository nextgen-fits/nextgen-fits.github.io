A few useful starting points

Before you choose.

What should I check before buying a minimalist monitor?

Measure the desk, choose a useful resolution and confirm the computer’s display output. A neat-looking frame will not fix a missing input or an unsuitable stand. Compare the screen and its connections together. Compare minimalist monitors →

DW-5600BB or GW-M5610U: which is the better everyday choice?

Start with GW-M5610U if light-powered charging is the priority. Choose DW-5600BB for its all-black look, after checking whether its negative display suits your viewing conditions. Radio reception on the GW-M5610U depends on location. Read the G-SHOCK comparison →

Does the BenQ GW2490 charge a laptop through USB-C?

No. The published video inputs are HDMI and DisplayPort. Plan laptop charging separately, and check adapters before assuming that one cable will carry video and power. Understand one-cable monitor setups →

Will a compact mouse suit an all-day desk?

Portability and hand support are different priorities. A slim mouse can travel easily; a larger shape may support your hand differently. Compare your grip, desk space and return options before choosing. Compare mouse fit and shape →
