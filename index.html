Questions worth asking

Before you buy.

What should I check when choosing a minimalist monitor?

Measure the usable desk space and stand footprint first. Then choose a resolution you can read comfortably and check the laptop's video output. USB-C is helpful when it carries the video and charging you need; a slim bezel alone won't sort the cables. Compare minimalist monitors →

Where should I start with an automatic watch under $200?

Start with wrist fit, the complete delivered price and a clearly identified model. Decide whether you enjoy winding and resetting a mechanical watch when it has been left unworn. If that sounds like a chore, compare quartz and solar too. Check current prices rather than assuming every listing stays below the budget. Build a budget automatic-watch shortlist →

How do I connect an external monitor to a laptop?

Match the laptop's video output to the monitor input, use a cable or adapter that supports the resolution you need, and select that input on the screen. Check USB-C video and laptop charging separately. A USB-C-shaped port does not automatically do both. Work through the laptop connection →

How should I choose a monitor for work and gaming?

Decide which job matters most. For documents, compare readable text, screen size and stand adjustment. For gaming, also check motion tests, supported refresh rates and the connection your computer or console can actually use. A high refresh-rate label cannot answer all of those questions. Compare work and gaming options →

How do I start a men's capsule wardrobe?

Look at the clothes you already reach for in a normal week. Measure a tee and trousers that fit, choose layers that work together, and fill the missing jobs. Fabric weight, garment length and the seller's size chart are more useful than guessing from a letter size. Plan a useful office wardrobe →

DW-5600BB or GW-M5610U: which G-SHOCK is easier to live with?

Start with GW-M5610U if solar charging and a positive display appeal to you. The DW-5600BB makes sense if you prefer its all-black look and can read the negative display comfortably. Keep the GW model exposed to suitable light, and check radio reception where you use it. Compare the two squares →

What is the difference between Seiko NH35/NH36 and 4R35/4R36 movements?

Mechanically, they are identical 24-jewel, 21,600 bph (3 Hz) automatic movements with 41 hours of power reserve, hacking seconds, and bidirectional Magic Lever winding built by the Seiko Group. Seiko stamps '4R35/4R36' on movements inside its own branded watches, while supplying the exact same architecture as 'TMI NH35A/NH36A' to microbrands and custom watch builders. Never overpay solely for the 4R36 rotor stamp—judge the complete watch by its crystal, water rating, and lug-to-lug fit. Read our NH36 vs 4R36 breakdown →

Why does macOS text look sharper on a 27-inch 4K monitor than on a 1440p QHD monitor?

Modern macOS removed subpixel antialiasing and optimizes font rendering around ~110 PPI (1x) or ~220 PPI (2x Retina). On a 27-inch 4K monitor (163 PPI), macOS renders a 5K (5120 × 2880) virtual canvas and downscales it cleanly to 4K ('Looks like 2560 × 1440'), delivering crisp, print-like characters. On a 27-inch QHD monitor (109 PPI), macOS renders at 1x native resolution, which can leave small code and spreadsheet fonts looking slightly soft. Compare 27-inch 4K vs QHD monitors →

Can I swim with a 100m water-resistant watch, or do I need a 200m diver?

A well-maintained 100m (10 bar) watch from Seiko, Citizen, or Casio handles lap swimming, pool plunges, and surface snorkeling safely as long as the crown is pushed fully in and the rubber gaskets are tested every 18–24 months. Stepping up to a 200m watch with a threaded screw-down crown (like the Orient Kamasu or Citizen Promaster Diver) locks the stem against accidental underwater bumps during ocean surf or scuba diving. Keep both 100m and 200m watches out of hot showers and saunas, where steam and soap degrade rubber seals. Read the 100m vs 200m water resistance guide →

What GSM fabric weight should I look for in a heavyweight men's T-shirt or hoodie?

For short-sleeve t-shirts, lightweight summer tees sit at 135–160 GSM, midweight everyday tees sit at 170–200 GSM, and true structured heavyweight tees (like the 229 GSM Carhartt K87 or 245 GSM Uniqlo U Crew) sit between 210 and 280 GSM. For hoodies, midweight three-season fleece sits at 280–340 GSM (8.5–10 oz), while winter heavyweight hoodies sit at 350–450+ GSM (10.5–13.5 oz). Explore the GSM fabric weight guide →

Do I need a Thunderbolt 4 dock or is a $25 USB-C hub enough for my laptop?

If you connect a single 1080p/QHD monitor (or a 4K monitor that already provides its own USB-C Power Delivery) and just need a couple of USB-A ports and an SD slot on the road, a $25–$45 USB-C hub is plenty. Choose a powered Thunderbolt 4 dock (40 Gbps, 90W–98W PD) when you want to drive dual 4K@60Hz monitors, plug in 2.5GbE wired Ethernet, and transfer large video files from NVMe SSDs over a single desktop cable.
