PART 5: Care & Maintenance — Making $15 Pieces Last 3 Years

Minimalism only works if your core garments do not degrade after three washes. High-end brands rely on gentle dry cleaning, but budget essentials thrive under smart home laundry habits. Follow these four rules to triple the lifespan of your wardrobe:

  • Rule 1: Always Wash Inside Out in Cold Water. Turning your Gildan hoodie and twill pants inside out before throwing them in the washing machine prevents surface friction. Cold water prevents dye bleed and fabric shrinkage.
  • Rule 2: Never High-Heat Dry Fleece. High dryer heat cooks polyester fibers, causing the fleece interior to feel scratchy. Hang dry your hoodies or use the "Air Fluff / Low Heat" setting.
  • Rule 3: Rotate Your Shoes. Leather sneakers need 24 hours between wears to air out internal foot moisture. Give your New Balance 608s a day off between heavy walking sessions.
  • Rule 4: Use Magic Erasers on White Midsoles. Keep a $3 pack of melamine foam erasers by your door. Wiping down the white soles of your sneakers once a week keeps them looking brand new indefinitely.
