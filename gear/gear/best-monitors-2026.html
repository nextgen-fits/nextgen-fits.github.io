Frequently Asked Questions

What is Dual-Mode technology and why does it matter in 2026?

Dual-Mode panels use dedicated hardware switching on the scalar to flip between native 4K resolution at 240Hz for cinematic fidelity, and 1080p resolution at 480Hz for competitive gaming. It gives you two specialized monitors in a single physical footprint.

Is OLED burn-in still an issue for everyday workspace use?

Modern third-generation OLEDs use graphene dissipation films, pixel shifting algorithms, and logo dimming to substantially reduce degradation risks. However, for users running non-stop 8-hour static spreadsheets with bright white backgrounds, full-array Mini-LED or IPS Black displays remain more practical long-term choices.

How much USB-C Power Delivery do I need to run a modern laptop?

Ultrabooks and standard 13-inch to 14-inch MacBooks run comfortably on 65W. Larger 15-inch and 16-inch laptops (like the MacBook Pro 16 or Dell XPS 15/16) under heavy editing or rendering loads require 90W to 96W to prevent battery drain while plugged in.
