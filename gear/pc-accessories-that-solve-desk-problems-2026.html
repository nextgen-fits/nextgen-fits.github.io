Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most essential accessory for a clean desk setup?

For visual cleanliness, a no-drill under-desk cable tray paired with a cable management box yields the highest immediate impact. For health and daily comfort, a monitor light bar like the BenQ ScreenBar is undisputed for relieving eye fatigue.

How do you manage cables on a standing desk without drilling holes?

Use clamp-on under-desk cable management trays (such as the Yecaye No-Drill Tray). They securely attach to the rear overhang of your tabletop via adjustable C-clamps, holding heavy power strips and wire bundles safely without damaging the wood.

Does a monitor light bar cause screen glare or reflections?

High quality monitor light bars like the BenQ ScreenBar use an asymmetrical optical design that casts illumination exclusively downward onto your desk surface, completely avoiding direct glare and reflections on the monitor glass.
