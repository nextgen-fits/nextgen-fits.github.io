Frequently Asked Questions

What's the single best desk accessory to start with?

If you only fix one thing, fix your lighting. A monitor light bar like the BenQ ScreenBar has the most noticeable day-to-day impact of anything on this list — reduced eye strain compounds over every single work session.

Do I need a cable management box AND a cable tray?

They solve different problems. The box hides your power strip and the tangle of chargers plugged into it, usually sitting on the floor or on the desk. The tray routes cables along the underside of the desk before they reach the floor. Most people start with the box alone and add the tray later if cables still visibly hang down.

Is a wireless charging stand actually faster than a cable?

No — a good USB-C cable will always charge faster than wireless. The point of a charging stand isn't speed, it's not having to find and plug in a cable every time you sit down, and not having a charging cable as one more thing cluttering the desk.
