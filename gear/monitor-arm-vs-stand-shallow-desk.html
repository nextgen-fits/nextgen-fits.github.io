Frequently asked questions

Will an arm fit any monitor?

No. Confirm the exact VESA pattern, monitor weight, screw depth and whether an adapter plate is required.

Can a clamp damage a desk?

It can if the top is weak, the clamp is overtightened or the edge is incompatible. Follow the desk and arm instructions.

Is an arm always more ergonomic?

No. It helps only if the adjustment range lets you place the display comfortably.
