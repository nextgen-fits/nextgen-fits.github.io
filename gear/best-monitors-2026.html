Frequently Asked Questions

What is Dual-Mode technology and why does it matter in 2026?

Dual-Mode panels use dedicated hardware switching on the scalar to flip between high resolution (4K at 165Hz) for cinematic fidelity, and lower resolutions at high refresh rates (such as 330Hz) for competitive gaming. It provides two specialized setups in a single display footprint.

Is OLED burn-in still an issue for everyday workspace use?

Modern third-generation OLEDs use pulsating heat pipes, graphite thermal films, and automatic logo dimming algorithms to significantly reduce degradation. However, for users running non-stop 8-hour static spreadsheets with bright white backgrounds, full-array Mini-LED or IPS Black displays remain more practical long-term options.

How much USB-C Power Delivery do I need to run a modern laptop?

Compact ultrabooks and standard 13-to-14-inch MacBooks run comfortably on 65W. Larger 15-to-16-inch creator laptops (like the MacBook Pro 16 or Dell XPS 16) under heavy video export or 3D rendering workloads require 90W to 98W to prevent battery drain while connected.
