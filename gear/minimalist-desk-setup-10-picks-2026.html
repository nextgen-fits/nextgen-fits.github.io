Frequently Asked Questions

What's the cheapest way to start a minimalist desk setup?

A single budget monitor, one monitor arm, and a desk mat gets you most of the visual impact for under $150. The mouse and standing desk are the easiest pieces to add later.

Do I need a monitor arm, or is a stand good enough?

A stand is fine if desk space isn't tight. An arm matters most when you want the monitor at eye level without a stack of books, or when you plan to add a second screen later — it frees the desk surface under the monitor entirely.

Is a $25 monitor arm actually reliable long-term?

Budget arms without a gas spring (like the WALI Single Monitor Mount) tend to hold their position more reliably over years than gas-spring arms, which can lose tension and slowly drift. If you want silky one-hand height adjustment, a gas-spring arm is worth the extra cost — just expect an occasional re-tighten down the line.

Is a leather desk mat worth it over a regular mouse pad?

Functionally, no — a desk mat and a mouse pad both let your mouse track smoothly. The desk mat's job is visual: it covers the gap between your keyboard and mouse so the desk reads as one considered surface instead of a pile of separate accessories.
