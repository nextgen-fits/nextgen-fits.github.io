Frequently asked questions

Does every USB-C port output video?

No. Confirm DisplayPort Alt Mode, Thunderbolt or the specific video feature in the laptop documentation.

Can the monitor charge my laptop?

Only if it provides USB-C Power Delivery at suitable wattage; check both the monitor rating and laptop needs.

Will one cable run two displays?

Possibly, depending on graphics support, dock technology, ports, operating system and display modes. Verify exact compatibility.
