Q1: Why does my desk setup still look messy even after buying a monitor arm?

A monitor arm clears screen space, but power strips and laptop charging bricks on the floor or desktop remain major visual clutter. You must conceal power blocks inside a dedicated cable management box and run cords vertically along the desk legs.

Q2: Are monitor light bars worth it compared to traditional desk lamps?

Yes, monitor light bars clamp directly onto the top bezel of your display, projecting asymmetric light onto your keyboard without creating glare or reflections on your screen while saving 100% of your desk footprint.
