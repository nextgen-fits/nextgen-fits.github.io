Frequently asked questions

What is the ideal monitor distance?

Use OSHA’s 20–40 inch range as a general starting point, then adjust for readable text and relaxed posture.

How deep should a desk be?

It depends on the screen, stand and your comfortable reach. Measure the setup instead of choosing a universal desk number.

Should the top of the screen be at eye level?

OSHA suggests the top at or slightly below eye level for many setups; glasses, posture and screen size can change the best fit.
