[ ] Confirm movement and finished-watch timing promise.
[ ] Identify warranty start date and covered faults.

[ ] Record return notice deadline and fit-check condition.
[ ] Confirm stock or preorder status and cancellation terms.
[ ] Calculate delivered cost and save written clarifications.
